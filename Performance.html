Performance Module

The LearnerTrack Performance Module is designed to deliver easily digestible, clear, headline management information, including reporting on Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and SFA Qualification achievement rates (QAR)

The 4 key performance module components are:
  • Performance Dashboard
  • Performance standard KPI reports
  • Bespoke local SAR reports
  • Performance cube
